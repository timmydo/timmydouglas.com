The newest Zelda game in the series was released just over a week
ago. There's not much I can say that hasn't already been said
somewhere else about it. It continues the same formula as the previous
one while making improvements to it. I'm probably only about halfway
through the game, but it's probably safe to say it will have its place
up there with the best games of all time.


One of the interesting things about it is that this blockbuster game
is only targeting the switch, which is basically nine-year-old phone
hardware. It's pretty amazing they are able to bring such a modern
game experience to such old hardware. On the other hand, you could
also claim that it's simpler to target a single piece of hardware
when modern PC games have to target different hardware and software
platforms.

I was hoping the kids could play it more when I'm done, but there is
still a lot of reading they'd have to do to understand where they are
supposed to go. Unfortunately a lot of the dialog is still not voiced,
which makes it more difficult for them because they can't read yet.
