Drew DeVault posted a new blog post titled [Please stop externalizing
your costs directly into my
face](https://drewdevault.com/2025/03/17/2025-03-17-Stop-externalizing-your-costs-on-me.html). He
calls out a couple of things that seem to have gotten worse in recent
years: bot and abusive traffic.

A lot of internet-era businesses are built on the economic model of
the user's being the product rather than the service provides. For
example, you have free search engines and various media platforms that
make money by selling advertisements or user information. It's just a
fact of life that if you give something out for free, people are going
to try to take as much as they can. There's no doubt that anyone with
a large enough platform is going to have people spending all their
time trying to keep costs down when it comes to serving abusive
traffic.

Then, you also have companies who have built a business model around
stopping abusive traffic, and that often comes at a price of blocking
certain users who are using less popular web browsers.

I'm left wondering if smaller companies like Drew's will be able to
operate easily on an internet in the future where providing a
free-tier is much more costly due to abusive anonymous traffic. It
would be sad to see all the free hosting provided by a few large
companies who can afford to take a hit on the free tier because they
can make it up with their profits through the volume of their user
base.
