title: "OpenAI's Sora"
---

OpenAI revealed Sora, a text-to-video model about a week ago. It might
be even more amazing than watching ChatGPT generate text and has the
potential to forever change video creation or film making. With some
more tweaks to the output format, it could probably generate some
pretty cool video game worlds as well.

If this takes off, does it even render actors obsolete? Famous ones
can command a pretty high salary, but an AI model isn't going to ask
for anything. It seems like a lot of genres would be forever changed
by a tool this powerful.

I think when I imagined the future of machine learning or AI tools a
couple of years ago, I was expecting robots and manufacturing
improvements, but these first waves of breakthroughs are really
focused around the creative arts.
